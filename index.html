The Book

What's broken in Eden is still working itself out today

We were made for more than we're living — to walk in abundance, to shape culture, and to pass on real faith, not just to a church but to whole nations. Somewhere along the way, that got lost. Treasure and Genius in Genesis goes back to where the unraveling started, and traces the thread of restoration God has been weaving ever since.

"Through inner healing and wholeness, rediscover your mandate to be fruitful, multiply, and have dominion."

This isn't only about personal healing, though it starts there. Dr. Heath makes the case that the same spirit of confusion described at Babel is rising again — and that God is raising up people ready to bring Kingdom wisdom into every sphere of culture, not with the world's weapons, but with restored identity and authority.

Seven spheres of influence

The book maps out where this restored authority is meant to take root — the arenas the author calls the nation's true culture-shapers.

The Author

A healing journey that became a message

Dr. Phil's own path into this material began in a hard season — a broken marriage, in the middle of a career as a mathematics teacher and researcher. In the wreckage of that separation, he gave his life to Christ, and came to see that he, too, was someone in need of deep healing.

That realization shaped everything after it. It gave him a lasting empathy for people carrying pain they haven't named, and it set him on the path of teaching the gospel and the hope of healing found in Christ — the same message now at the center of Treasure and Genius in Genesis.
